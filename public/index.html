Guidelines & Privacy

How C.A.R.E. Hub keeps this space safe and anonymous.

Community standards

    How your privacy is protected

    • No account and no IP address are stored. Your "Student #" handle comes from a random key kept only in this browser.
    • Names, phone numbers, emails, student IDs and social handles are replaced with tags like [Student A] before anything is saved.
    • A summary of every concern you share, with names removed, always goes to the Guidance team, whether it is posted on the feed or sent privately. Chats with the C.A.R.E. Adviser are not saved unless you choose to post or send them.
    • Urgent reports (threats, harassment, self-harm) skip the public feed and go privately to counselors.
    • Photos are only ever seen by counselors, with location metadata removed.
    • Similar reports are grouped into concerns so counselors can act. Concerns show counts, never your words.

    What this is - and isn't

    C.A.R.E. Hub is an incident detection and support bridge. It is not a replacement for formal student handbook disciplinary proceedings, law enforcement reports, or professional psychological counseling. Reports describe concerns and never determine who is at fault.

    Need someone to talk to?

    You don't have to handle things alone. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. You can also talk to any trusted adult nearby.
