### Strict Builder Guardrails
1. **No Accusations or Defamation:** The platform must never facilitate public shaming, witch hunts, or call-out culture. Posts identifying individuals as perpetrators must be redacted or held for moderation.
2. **No Determining Guilt or Fault:** Terminology must remain neutral, factual, and non-judgmental (e.g., *"Reported incident"*, *"Pattern observed"*, *"Forwarded for counselor review"*).
3. **No Retaliation Facilitation:** No comment sections or counter-campaign features. Only supportive reactions are permitted.
4. **Synthetic Data Only for Evaluation:** Demo environments must strictly utilize synthetic data. Never store or process real student names or actual complaints.
5. **No Policy / Legal Substitution:** Prominent disclaimers must clarify that C.A.R.E. Hub is an incident detection and support bridge, not a substitute for formal handbook disciplinary proceedings, law enforcement reports, or professional healthcare.
6. **Persistent Crisis Access:** Emergency numbers (e.g., NCMH 1553, 911) must remain accessible from both the navigation header and sidebars at all times.
7. **Support Pathways & Transparency:** The adviser may suggest local support (student council, class adviser) for low-severity concerns, in addition to, never instead of, formal channels. For serious cases it must always point to the guidance office, the relevant authorities and the crisis hotlines. Students are told upfront that a sanitized summary of each concern is always shared with the guidance/admin page.

**Strict Anti-SaaS Design Rules:**
1. **NO Marketing Hero Banners or Slogans:** Never display oversized headlines (e.g., *"A safer campus starts with being heard"*), commercial CTAs, or promotional marketing blocks.
2. **NO Corporate Graphics or Clutter:** Strip out vector shields, commercial clip-art, marketing feature grids, or promotional fluff.
3. **NO Public Vanity Metrics on the Feed:** Never display large vanity stat cards (*"320+ experiences shared"*) on the main view. The student's immediate focus upon opening the app must be the **live community feed**.
4. **NO User Accounts, Logins, or Passwords:** Zero student accounts, school email verifications, or IP address tracking. The platform operates on client-generated ephemeral tokens.
5. **Calm, Student-Centric Aesthetics:** Use a soothing, modern color palette: Slate neutrals (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`, `#f8fafc`) with calm teal/emerald accents (`#0d9488`, `#14b8a6`). Avoid harsh neon cyan-and-black contrasts. Support clean light and dark modes with high legibility.
